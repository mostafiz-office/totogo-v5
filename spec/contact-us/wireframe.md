## Tablet (768px)

- Hero stacks: text first, photo below.
- Topic cards: 2×2 grid.
- Form fields: single column except name/phone row may stay 2-up if space allows.

## Mobile (360px)

- Hero: text, photo, CTA stacked full width.
- Topic cards: swipeable slider (shared `SolutionSlider`), one card visible with peek.
- Form: single column, 44px inputs, full-width submit button.
- Direct contact card full width.
- Final CTA: centered text, full-width button.
